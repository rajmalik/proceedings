About

We build AI products, and we build them for you.

Krishes is an independent AI-powered software company. We ship our own AI products, starting with Meridian, a community-driven immigration companion on the App Store. We bring the same team, stack and standard to client work. Everything we sell, we run in production ourselves.

Community-driven AI products.

The best AI products don't just answer questions. They connect the people asking them.

We design products where a model grounds itself in what a real community has lived through, and where users find each other, not just an answer. Meridian is the proof: grounded, cited answers over real applicant experiences, plus cohorts of people going through the same process at the same time. We build that pattern for other domains where people are navigating something hard, together.

Gemini-powered AI offerings.

Retrieval that actually retrieves, and generation you can defend.

We build grounded RAG on Vertex AI Search, structured extraction and auto-tagging with controlled vocabularies, AI onboarding flows, and content moderation pipelines. Guardrails, evaluation and citations are built in from the start, not bolted on. We've shipped all of it under the constraints of a regulated domain, where a wrong answer isn't an inconvenience.

GCP migration.

We run production workloads on Cloud Run, Firestore, Vertex AI, Cloud Build and IAM every day.

We'll migrate you onto that stack with the parts teams usually discover too late: manual-approval deploys, no-traffic candidate revisions and smoke tests before promotion, externalized configuration that changes without a redeploy, and immutable, component-scoped release tags so you can always roll back to exactly what was running.

We don't consult on this stack. We ship on it.
