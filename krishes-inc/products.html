Meridian

AI companion for the U.S. immigration journey

Meridian is an AI companion for the US immigration journey. Ask a question and get a grounded, cited answer drawn from what real applicants have posted: sourced, never legal advice. Then find the people in your exact situation: Meridian organizes applicants into timeline cohorts by case type and filing month, so H-1B, STEM OPT and green-card filers can compare receipt dates, RFEs, service centers and approvals as they land. Instead of scrolling forums hoping to find someone who filed the month you did, you see the whole cohort at a glance.
