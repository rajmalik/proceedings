Gemini AI Solutions

AI you can defend to a user, a lawyer, or a regulator.

We build grounded AI systems on Vertex AI and Gemini, where every answer traces back to a source. Retrieval that actually retrieves, generation that cites, and guardrails written into the prompt rather than bolted on afterwards.

  • Grounded RAG on Vertex AI Search, with citations and relevance tuning
  • Structured extraction and auto-tagging against a controlled vocabulary
  • Conversational onboarding and intent classification, with deterministic fallbacks
  • Content moderation pipelines, wordlist plus model
  • Evaluation and guardrails designed in from the first commit
