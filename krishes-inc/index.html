AI craft

Retrieval and reasoning that stays grounded.

We build the AI, not just call it. A Python / FastAPI backend grounds answers on a Google Vertex AI Search datastore and generates them with Gemini: guardrails, source citations, a matching engine, automated tagging, and content moderation.

  • Grounded retrieval on Vertex AI Search (Discovery Engine)
  • Gemini for answers, tagging, and moderation
  • A scored matching engine over structured profiles
  • Firestore, BigQuery, and Cloud Storage for data
